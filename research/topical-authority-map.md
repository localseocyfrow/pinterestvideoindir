## 0. What changed in this refresh (2026-08-02)

### ⚠️ Correction: the July volumes were NOT stale

An earlier note in this project claimed the pillar had grown from 223,000 to
270,000/mo. **That was a measurement error, not growth.** Ahrefs exposes two
different volume metrics and they were being compared directly:

| Source | `volume_mode` | Definition | `pinterest video indir` |
|--------|---------------|------------|------------------------|
| Keywords Explorer overview | — | avg. monthly over last 12 months | **223,000** |
| Site Explorer organic keywords | `average` | avg. monthly over last 12 months | **237,000** |
| Site Explorer organic keywords | `monthly` (default) | **latest month only** | **270,000** |

All three were pulled on the same date. The 270,000 figure is the latest single
month — a seasonal reading, not a trend. **The 12-month average is unchanged
from July.** Always compare like with like; when quoting a volume in this file,
it is the **12-month average** unless stated otherwise.

### Genuine changes since 2026-07-13

1. **`sss pinterest` changed parent topic** → now `pinterest video indir`
   (was treated as its own cluster). This materially weakens the case for a
   separate `/sss-pinterest/` landing page — see C10 below.
2. **Keyword Difficulty dropped across the core cluster**: indirici 6 → **0**,
   galeriye 11 → **0**, resim 4 → **0**. The category got easier, not harder.
3. **Competitor set shifted**: `toolsmart.ai` roughly doubled (23k → 47k),
   `pindown.io` grew (54k → 70k), `klickpin.com` lost authority (DR 26 → 17),
   and `mylifebox.com` (38.6k) + `convertico.com` are new entrants.
   `indirpin.tr` (sister domain) dropped out of the top 15.
4. **`pinterest karusel indir` returns no measurable volume** — our page exists
   and is fine, but the cluster should not receive further investment.
5. **Two clusters closed** since July: `/pinterest-nedir/` and
   `/pinterest-coktu-mu/` were built on 2026-08-02.

## 1. Methodology

Clusters are built from Ahrefs Keywords Explorer and validated against the
**parent topic** field, which is the decisive anti-cannibalisation signal: if a
keyword's parent topic is `pinterest video indir`, it belongs on the pillar or
an existing spoke, *not* on a new URL — regardless of how attractive its volume
looks. Competitor data comes from Site Explorer (organic keywords + organic
competitors). Priority = demand × ease (inverse KD) × strategic gap.

### The single most important competitive fact

**Every one of `indirpin.com.tr`'s top 30 keywords ranks on the bare homepage**
(`https://indirpin.com.tr/`) — including `sss pinterest` (10,000),
`pinterest video indir galeriye` (4,300) and `pinterest video kaydetme`
(2,000). Their entire ~139k traffic sits on **one URL** with no supporting-page
footprint.

**Implication:** they are near-unassailable on the bare head terms (DR 23 but
years of topical/link equity on one page), and structurally undefended on every
informational, troubleshooting and definitional intent. Our 41-page footprint is
the correct strategy — keep widening the surface they do not cover rather than
attacking the homepage head-on.

## 8. Content quality guardrails (unchanged, still binding)

Every page: unique H1 + intent-specific intro, Kısa Cevap answer block
(40–70 words, paired with an explicit question), substantive body ≥ 400 words of
original Turkish, page-specific FAQ set that duplicates **no** question already
live on the site, FAQPage schema matching the visible FAQs exactly,
BreadcrumbList + Article (or WebApplication where a real downloader is visible),
trailing-slash canonical, and both standing notices:

- `Pinterest ile resmi bir bağlantımız yoktur.`
- `Yalnızca size ait olan veya indirme/kullanma iznine sahip olduğunuz içerikleri kaydetmenizi öneririz.`

Never publish: fabricated ratings, reviews, download counts, live outage status
or any statistic we cannot verify.
